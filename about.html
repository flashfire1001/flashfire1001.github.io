About

A longer introduction to what I study, what I build, and the questions that keep me curious.

Hello, I’m Jimmy.

I’m a Computer Engineering student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I’m drawn to systems, high-performance computing, databases, and AI—especially the places where a concrete implementation exposes something that an abstract explanation hides.

What I care about

I like tools that make complicated structures easier to inspect: a debugger that connects source to assembly, a visual map that reveals how research evolves, or a set of notes that remains useful after an exam.

How I work

I prefer building a small coherent version first, observing what is actually confusing, and then expanding it. The same principle guides this website: the content can change freely, while a stable visual system keeps it together.

Beyond the work

I write about technical ideas, learning workflows, and the occasional question that does not fit neatly into a project page. You can find the structured details in Education, download my CV, or get in touch.
